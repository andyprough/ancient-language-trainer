Screenshot Pair Helper

Offline experimental helper for Ancient Language Trainer - no OCR, no internet, no transcription

Vocabulary screenshot → suggested paired crops → manual review → ZIP export

1. Choose your textbook screenshot

This helper does not modify your main Ancient Language Trainer collection. It exports paired PNG images that you can add through the existing vocabulary editor. All processing is local to this browser tab.

Use the numbered suggestions as a starting point, not as verified vocabulary.
Choose an image to begin.

2. Review or correct the crop rectangles

Choose a pair below, then select Draw English crop or Draw Hebrew crop. Drag a rectangle around the desired portion of the original page. The blue rectangle indicates English; orange indicates Hebrew. You can remove unwanted suggestions and add missed ones.

Hebrew suggestions include a little extra room at the left to preserve short dashes. If your textbook uses unusual spacing or extra leader dots appear, manually adjust the rectangles before export.

3. Approve the suggested pairs

The ZIP contains numbered PNG word/definition images and a manifest. No OCR or cloud upload.
